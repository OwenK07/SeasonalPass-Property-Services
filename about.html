Our story

A local answer to the weekend chore list.

SeasonalPass started with a familiar Halifax feeling: after a full work week, there was still a full weekend of property work waiting at home. Busy families needed more than another contractor to call. They needed a dependable local team who could care for their property through changing seasons.

Our mission is to help West End and Northwest Arm homeowners spend less time coordinating upkeep and more time enjoying home, family, and the neighbourhood. We build lasting relationships, plan for the seasons ahead, and make the cost easy to understand month after month.

Small by design

Why we cap annual subscriptions.

We grow around the homes our local team can care for well—not around a target to serve as many addresses as possible.

Capacity we can stand behind
